# THE WORKSHOP v9.10.0 — Find Your Way In

v9.10.0 makes it easier to find a useful place to participate without turning THE WORKSHOP into an engagement feed.

## Where I Could Help

Signed-in members now see active Open Benches from other makers on Home. Each Project includes a plain-language reason it may be relevant, drawn from the skills, tools, help areas, and learning goals the member chose to list on My Bench.

These are transparent connections, not recommendations from an opaque score. Projects remain ordered by their latest meaningful update.

## Filter the open work

The Builds page can filter Open Benches by all six invitation types:

- Feedback Welcome
- Need a Hand
- Looking for Tester
- Open to Collaborator
- Need a Tool or Material
- Make a Variation

Signed-in members can also select **Matches My Bench**. Empty-filter states are explicit, and the full list remains available in one click.

## Search more of the Workshop

Unified search now includes dedicated **Open Benches** and **Notes & Logs** views. Open Bench request text is searchable, Project logs and published Shop Notes share one practical search surface, and Help search once again includes troubleshooting questions.

No new top-level module, public score, ranking, streak, popularity total, or infinite feed was added.
