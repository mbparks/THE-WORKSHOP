# THE WORKSHOP v9.6.0 — Activity & Belonging

Phase 3 makes THE WORKSHOP feel inhabited without turning it into a conventional social feed.

## Around the Workshop
Home now includes a compact recent-activity section for meaningful maker activity such as project notebook updates, Project Talk, Maker Crew meetups, and Community Build team formation. It is deliberately short and artifact-centered.

## Working On
Members can publish a short-lived Working On status from Edit Bench. Statuses last 7 or 14 days and then disappear automatically. They surface on the member Bench, People, and Maker Crew member cards.

## Crew Board
The existing Crew bulletin system is promoted into a clearer Crew Board destination in Maker Crew local navigation. It remains short-lived, local, and useful rather than becoming a chat system.

## Live attendance
Regular Live events support Interested and I’m Going. Event pages show who is joining, using callsigns where available. Attendance also surfaces on the Live + Calendar overview.

## Maker Variations
Community Builds now give completed maker interpretations more visibility through a Recent Maker Variations showcase. Variations remain explicitly noncompetitive.

No generic social feed, follower counts, or private messaging were added.
