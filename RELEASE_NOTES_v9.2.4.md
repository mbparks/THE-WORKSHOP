# THE WORKSHOP v9.2.4 — Community-First Language

This release performs a community-first language pass across THE WORKSHOP.

The central Crew Work message is now:

> Crew Projects, contributions, and requests are ways to help makers, clients, and users solve problems, share knowledge, and grow together.

GearHead membership and participation are now described in terms of expanding shared knowledge, useful resources, collaboration, learning, and opportunities for the broader Workshop community. Green Shoe Garage remains visible where it is appropriate as the operator/brand, source attribution, repository owner, or project/resource origin.

No permissions, billing behavior, routes, or underlying data models were changed.
