# Community Code of Conduct

## The short version

**Don't be an idiot.**

THE WORKSHOP is a place for constructive making, learning, repair, experimentation, critique, and technical disagreement.

Participants are expected to:

- critique ideas and designs without attacking people;
- give useful, specific feedback;
- respect creators, attribution, and licenses;
- avoid harassment, hate, threats, stalking, doxxing, spam, scams, predatory behavior, plagiarism, and unsafe encouragement;
- protect private information and do not pressure others to disclose contact or exact location data;
- respect Maker Crew and private-meetup boundaries;
- treat failed experiments as useful knowledge rather than ammunition for ridicule;
- use ordinary judgment around tools, materials, meetups, and exchanges.

Moderators may remove content, restrict participation, suspend or ban accounts, or escalate matters when necessary to preserve a safe and constructive workshop.

See [`TERMS.md`](TERMS.md) for the full Terms & Community Conduct.
