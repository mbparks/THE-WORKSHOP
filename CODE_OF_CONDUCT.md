# Community Code of Conduct

THE WORKSHOP is a place for constructive making, learning, repair, experimentation, critique, and technical disagreement.

Participants are expected to:

- critique ideas and designs without attacking people;
- give useful, specific feedback;
- respect creators, attribution, and licenses;
- avoid harassment, hate, spam, predatory behavior, plagiarism, and unsafe encouragement;
- protect private information and do not pressure others to disclose contact or location data;
- treat failed experiments as useful knowledge rather than ammunition for ridicule.

Moderators may remove content, restrict participation, or act on accounts when necessary to preserve a safe and constructive workshop.
