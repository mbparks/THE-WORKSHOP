01 · YOUR WORKSHOP

Return to the work—not a feed.

Home helps a maker resume an active project, see a small amount of meaningful activity around the community, find an upcoming session, and discover work that fits their Bench.

  • Resume your own active project
  • Around the Workshop activity
  • Recommended and stretch builds
  • Upcoming Crew and Live activity
  • Cross-disciplinary discovery

02 · MY BENCH + MAKER ID

Your practice, tools, learning, and current work.

A Bench belongs equally to a programmer, ceramicist, sewist, electronics maker, photographer, repairer, machinist, illustrator, or multidisciplinary maker.

  • Unique public callsign
  • Craft Path and Maker ID
  • Temporary Working On status
  • Practices, tools, software, and platforms
  • Can Help With and Want to Learn
  • Local Maker Crew affiliation

03 · PROJECTS

Keep the decisions, evidence, wrong turns, and result together.

Projects are the main social objects in THE WORKSHOP. A Project can be a physical build, application, repair, experiment, artwork, recipe, performance, research effort, or something that does not fit a category yet.

  • Overview, Guided Build, Notebook, files, team, and details
  • Physical, software, and electronics context
  • Public, Members, GearHead, and Private visibility
  • Build Fit and Make It Yours
  • Versioned files, media, releases, and GitHub context
  • Completion and Maker Variations

04 · INTERACTION AROUND THE WORK

Talk, follow, mention, ask, and collaborate in context.

Interaction stays attached to useful work rather than becoming a popularity contest. Callsigns make people addressable while Projects give the conversation a reason to exist.

  • Project Talk with one-level replies
  • @callsign mentions and restrained notifications
  • Follow a Project—not a person count
  • Ask with Project Context
  • Callsign-based collaborator invitations
  • Visible Made By / With credits and project roles

05 · COMMUNITY BUILDS

Start from a shared idea. Build alone or form a team.

Prompts, briefs, sessions, build-alongs, teardowns, and weekly questions live together as Community Builds. The emphasis is interpretation and shared learning—not winners.

  • Prompts, Build Alongs, Briefs, Sessions, and Teardowns
  • Build Fit against your own Bench
  • Small callsign-based project teams
  • Solo participation
  • Maker Variations from completed interpretations
  • Noncompetitive community participation

06 · HELP + CRITIQUE

Ask for troubleshooting, critique, or identification help.

Help requests can carry Project evidence, tools, materials, the latest Notebook entry, and files. THE WORKSHOP can surface people whose explicit skills may fit the question without ranking them.

  • Troubleshooting questions
  • Design critique requests
  • What Is This? identification
  • Possible makers who can help
  • Callsign mentions in questions and answers
  • Project-linked context and evidence

07 · PEOPLE + SKILL EXCHANGE

Find someone through what they know, need, or are making.

People are not reduced to follower totals. Callsigns, Working On statuses, practices, and reciprocal skill matches help makers begin a useful conversation.

  • Unique callsigns in one global identity namespace
  • Search by practice, tool, platform, or skill
  • Can Help With / Want to Learn matching
  • Ask This Maker
  • Mute and block controls
  • Profile and location privacy

08 · MAKER CREWS

A local bulletin board, workshop map, and place to organize.

Maker Crews connect people locally without publishing member home locations. A Crew has a unique handle, approximate regional map point, local Board, Meetups, shared Bench, exchange, and organizer Studio.

  • Globally unique Crew handles
  • Workshop Map using approximate Crew regions
  • Crew Board, Meetups, Sessions, and Announcements
  • Local Scrap and skill exchange
  • Coverage ZIPs and starred map ZIP
  • Crew Studio roles and editable full-precision LAT/LON

09 · LIVE + CALENDAR

One answer to “what is happening and when?”

Live broadcasts, Project Clinics, Community Build sessions, Maker Crew events, and eligible GearHead events can share one calendar layer.

  • Upcoming and archived events
  • Online and in-person context
  • Interested and I’m Going attendance
  • Visible attendee callsigns
  • Linked Projects and Community Builds
  • ICS calendar export

10 · SHOP MANUAL

Shared knowledge with provenance and evidence.

The Shop Manual holds guides, references, patterns, field notes, and reusable knowledge. Entries can show who curated or tested them and which real Projects used the information.

  • Guides, references, templates, and patterns
  • Tools, materials, time, and difficulty
  • Curated By and Built/Tested By
  • Related Workshop Projects
  • Licensing and reusable files
  • Search and discipline filters

11 · GEARHEAD CREW

Deeper resources and shared work for supporting members.

GearHead Crew membership opens deeper tutorials, files, early releases, Crew Work, Vault material, feedback opportunities, and After Hours sessions while the main Workshop community remains open to everyone.

  • Crew Work and collaboration opportunities
  • File Vault and revisioned resources
  • Deep dives, shop films, and tutorials
  • Early Access feedback
  • After Hours sessions
  • Monthly or annual membership

12 · APPEARANCE, OFFLINE WORK + PRIVACY

Make the environment yours and keep control of your work.

THE WORKSHOP supports light, dark, and high-contrast presentation; Focused or Detailed density; animated, quiet, or disabled atmosphere; local draft recovery; and reviewable queued offline work.

  • Theme, density, and atmosphere controls
  • Reduced motion and high contrast support
  • Local draft recovery and Guided Build progress
  • Offline Work review, retry, discard, and sync
  • Version/update diagnostics
  • No first-party advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies
