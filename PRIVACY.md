# THE WORKSHOP — Privacy Policy

THE WORKSHOP uses account information to operate the maker community and local browser storage to provide features members choose to use. It does not include first-party advertising or behavioral-analytics cookies.

## Account and community data

THE WORKSHOP stores the information needed to operate your account and the things you choose to create or share, including profile details, projects, Workshop activity, Maker Crew participation, saved items, files, and privacy settings. Visibility controls determine which community content is public, member-only, GearHead-only, or private.

## Location

Maker Crew map pins represent a Crew region or a manually chosen Crew marker. The map is not intended to publish member home locations or private meetup addresses.

## Cookies & Local Storage

**Session cookie.** When you sign in, THE WORKSHOP sets one first-party cookie named `workshop_session`. It is used only to keep you signed in and associate requests with your account. The cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, marked Secure in production, and expires after 30 days unless you sign out sooner.

**Local browser storage.** THE WORKSHOP uses your browser's local storage for user-facing functionality such as appearance and Workshop Atmosphere preferences, Focused/Detailed view, draft/autosave recovery, Guided Build progress, offline caches, and queued offline work. This information stays in that browser unless a feature explicitly synchronizes data with your Workshop account.

THE WORKSHOP does not use first-party advertising, marketing, or behavioral-tracking cookies. External services you intentionally open or load may have their own privacy and storage practices.

## Your controls

You can change content visibility inside THE WORKSHOP, sign out to end the current session, clear local browser storage through your browser, export your account data, or use the available account-removal controls. Clearing local storage may remove unsynchronized drafts, offline work, or local preferences.

Community privacy expectations and acceptable conduct are covered separately in `TERMS.md`.


## Public Workshop Addresses

If you claim a callsign or manage a Maker Crew handle, that `@address` is intended to be a public Workshop identifier. It does not expose your email address, legal name, precise location, or private Bench content. Callsigns and handles share one global namespace. Retired addresses are held for 30 days before becoming available for reuse.
