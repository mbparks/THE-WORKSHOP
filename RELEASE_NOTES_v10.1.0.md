# THE WORKSHOP v10.1.0 — Unfinished in Public

v10.1 makes incomplete work easier to share without turning it into performance.

## Unfinished snapshots

From any Project you own or collaborate on, share a compact working snapshot:

- what exists;
- what is stuck;
- the next tiny step; and
- what help would matter.

The snapshot can be published, kept for Workshop members, held privately, or saved as a draft. Public snapshots appear in the existing **Make Together** hub in most-recently-updated order. They remain attached to the Project, and do not expose follower counts, popularity totals, rankings, streaks, or private request details.

## Safety and continuity

Visibility is enforced on every read path. Drafts and private snapshots are excluded from the public gallery; project visibility and member blocks continue to govern access. Owners and collaborators can revise or remove their own snapshots without deleting the Project or its Notebook history.

## Scope guardrails

v10.1 adds no new primary navigation module. The next roadmap releases remain v10.2 Commons, v10.3 Handoff Field Cards, v10.4 Gather Kits, v10.5 Local Quests, and v10.6 Connected Workshop.
