# THE WORKSHOP v9.12.0 — Open Bench Hours

v9.12.0 turns an open invitation into a real, bounded time when makers can work alongside one another.

## Declare when the Bench is open

The owner of an active Open Bench Project can schedule a working window with:

- a concrete topic;
- start time and duration;
- timezone and meeting format;
- a public note about what to bring, read, test, or discuss;
- private connection details for accepted makers;
- a small capacity used only to prevent overbooking.

Upcoming windows appear soonest first on the Project and Home. Ended windows close automatically, and a host can cancel an upcoming hour.

## Coordinate without publishing attendance

A signed-in maker can privately request a place and explain what would make the hour useful. The host can accept or decline from the Project. The requesting maker can withdraw.

Meeting links, contact methods, rooms, and arrival instructions remain hidden from visitors and pending requests. They are returned only to the host and accepted makers. Public cards show only whether space is available or full—never capacity, attendance, or demand counts.

Request, decision, and cancellation notifications are restrained and action-oriented. Routine scheduling does not create broadcast notifications.

## Reuse the Workshop calendar

Hours a maker hosts or has been accepted into appear in the existing Live + Calendar surface and its ICS export. Open Bench Hours do not create another module, feed, or calendar.

The release adds no likes, leaderboard, popularity score, streak, opaque ranking, public attendance total, or artificial urgency.
