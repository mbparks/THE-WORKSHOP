# THE WORKSHOP v9.2.5 — Books + Commerce Channels

THE WORKSHOP now presents Etsy, RedBubble, and Amazon Books together as complementary external commerce and discovery channels. Etsy supports digital designs and useful files, RedBubble carries art and merchandise, and Amazon Books supports longer-form knowledge. The About page explicitly frames these channels as both modest revenue sources and ways for new makers, clients, and users to discover the work and community.

No new module was added.
