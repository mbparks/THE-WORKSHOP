# THE WORKSHOP v9.9.0 — Bench Handshakes

v9.9.0 turns an Open Bench invitation into a small, accountable collaboration loop.

## Extend a handshake

A signed-in maker can choose one of the Project’s active Open Bench signals and describe a specific offer: a test they can run, feedback they can give, a tool they can lend, or work they can share. The offer becomes a visible part of the Project instead of disappearing into a generic comment stream.

Only one active offer per maker, Project, and signal is allowed. Project owners manage the invitation itself and cannot respond to their own invitation.

## Close the loop

Project owners can move each offer through three useful outcomes:

- **Handshake made** — the offer is acknowledged;
- **Help landed** — the contribution happened;
- **Not this time** — the offer is respectfully closed.

An optional owner note records what should happen next or what the offer contributed. The maker who extended an active handshake can withdraw it.

## Keep it project-centered

Bench Handshakes inherit Project visibility and remain attached to the work. Notifications happen only when a maker extends an offer or the Project owner changes its status. There are no public response totals, likes, rankings, streaks, recommendation scoring, or separate engagement feed.
