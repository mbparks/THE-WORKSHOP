# THE WORKSHOP v9.2.1 — Privacy Disclosure

This maintenance release adds a plain-language public Privacy Policy and a concise **Cookies & Local Storage** section.

The policy identifies `workshop_session` as THE WORKSHOP's essential first-party sign-in cookie and explains the browser-local storage used for appearance preferences, Workshop Atmosphere, Focused/Detailed view, draft recovery, Guided Build progress, offline caches, and queued offline work. It also states that THE WORKSHOP does not use first-party advertising, marketing, or behavioral-tracking cookies.

No account, content, project, membership, or privacy-control behavior changes in this release.
