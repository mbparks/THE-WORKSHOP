# THE WORKSHOP — Terms & Community Conduct

**Terms version: 2026-08-16**

## The short version

**Don’t be an idiot.**

Make things. Share what you learn. Disagree about ideas without being awful to people. Do not use THE WORKSHOP to hurt, exploit, deceive, harass, or endanger anyone.

## 1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. By creating or continuing to use an account, you confirm that you meet that requirement and that the information you provide is reasonably accurate.

## 2. Be decent

No harassment, hate, threats, stalking, doxxing, predatory behavior, impersonation, targeted abuse, spam, scams, or deliberate disruption. Technical disagreement and blunt critique are welcome; personal attacks are not.

## 3. Keep it lawful and reasonably safe

Do not use THE WORKSHOP to plan, facilitate, encourage, or distribute unlawful activity or malicious content. Making carries real physical risk. You are responsible for your own tools, materials, workspace, protective equipment, procedures, and judgment. Community posts are not a substitute for qualified engineering, medical, legal, safety, or other professional advice.

## 4. Respect privacy

Do not publish another person’s private information without permission. Do not pressure members to disclose home addresses, exact locations, personal contact information, or private meetup details. Crew and meetup privacy controls must be respected.

You keep ownership of content you create. By posting it, you give THE WORKSHOP a non-exclusive permission to host, copy, display, format, and distribute that content only as reasonably needed to operate, moderate, back up, and present the service. Only upload material you have the right to share. Respect attribution, licenses, trademarks, copyrights, and other creators’ rights.

## 6. Moderation

THE WORKSHOP may remove or restrict content, limit features, suspend accounts, ban accounts, or permanently anonymize/remove login access when reasonably necessary to protect the community, enforce these terms, respond to abuse, or comply with legal obligations. Moderation decisions may consider context, pattern, severity, safety, and prior behavior.

## 7. Local meetups and exchanges

Maker Crew events, tool help, Scrap Bin exchanges, and other in-person activity are arranged between participants. THE WORKSHOP does not inspect venues, tools, people, materials, or transactions. Use ordinary judgment, protect private addresses, meet in appropriate places, and leave any situation that feels unsafe.

## 8. Accounts and security

Keep your credentials secure and do not share access to your account. You are responsible for activity performed through your account unless you promptly report unauthorized access. Administrators may invalidate sessions or require password resets when account security is in question.

THE WORKSHOP is provided on an as-available basis. Features may change, break, be removed, or be unavailable. Community information can be incomplete, experimental, outdated, or wrong. Verify anything important before relying on it, especially where safety, money, property, or legal obligations are involved.

Use the in-app reporting and moderation tools for harmful content, member conduct, account abuse, or safety concerns. Copyright or other rights complaints should identify the material, the right involved, and enough information for the operator to investigate.
