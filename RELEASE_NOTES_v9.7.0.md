# THE WORKSHOP v9.7.0 — First Visit and Discovery

v9.7.0 gives a first-time visitor a useful path through THE WORKSHOP before asking them to create an account.

## Find Your First Thing

- Choose from Electronics, Repair, Software, Textiles, Art + Image, Mechanisms, and Experiments.
- Receive three public projects, one Community Build, and one maker or Maker Crew.
- Choices are stored only in the visitor’s browser and simply reorder public Workshop material.
- Every result opens directly without sign-in.

## Clearer public entryways

- The logged-out homepage now presents Tour, First Thing, Benches, and Maker Crews as four explicit ways in.
- The tour ends at Find Your First Thing rather than a sign-in decision.
- Public project pages include a Start Here guide for the overview, Notebook, Guided Build, and files.
- Membership language explains that an account is needed only when a visitor chooses to contribute.

The release adds no popularity ranking, engagement streak, behavioral tracking, or new social feed.
